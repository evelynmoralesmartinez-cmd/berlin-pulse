# Activities for children in Berlin. One activity per paragraph. DRAFT by Claude – to be verified by Eve.
# Panoramas para niños en Berlín. Una actividad por párrafo. BORRADOR de Claude – por verificar.

Activity for kids in Berlin – Berlin Zoo and Aquarium: The Berlin Zoo (Zoologischer Garten) is the oldest zoo in Germany and is located in the city centre, next to Tiergarten. The Aquarium next door shows fish, reptiles and insects. Good for all ages, mostly outdoors.

Activity for kids in Berlin – Tierpark Berlin: Tierpark Berlin in the Lichtenberg district is one of the largest zoos in Europe by area, with lots of space to walk. Good for all ages, outdoors.

Activity for kids in Berlin – German Museum of Technology (Deutsches Technikmuseum): The German Museum of Technology in Kreuzberg shows real trains, planes, ships and cars. Its hands-on science centre Spectrum has many experiments for children. Good for school-age children, indoors.

Activity for kids in Berlin – Natural History Museum (Museum für Naturkunde): The Natural History Museum in Mitte is famous for its huge dinosaur skeletons. Good for all ages, indoors.

Activity for kids in Berlin – Labyrinth Kindermuseum: Labyrinth Kindermuseum in the Wedding area is a hands-on museum where children learn by playing, climbing and building. Best for children of about 3 to 11 years, indoors.

Activity for kids in Berlin – LEGOLAND Discovery Centre: The LEGOLAND Discovery Centre at Potsdamer Platz is an indoor LEGO attraction with rides, building areas and a 4D cinema. Best for children of about 3 to 10 years, indoors.

Activity for kids in Berlin – FEZ-Berlin: FEZ-Berlin in Wuhlheide park (Treptow-Köpenick) is one of the largest leisure centres for children and young people in Europe, with indoor and outdoor activities.

Activities for kids in Berlin on a rainy day: Good indoor options for children in Berlin are the German Museum of Technology, the Natural History Museum, Labyrinth Kindermuseum and the LEGOLAND Discovery Centre.

Playgrounds and parks for kids in Berlin: Tempelhofer Feld is great for cycling, skating and flying kites. Britzer Garten in Neukölln has large playgrounds, lakes and a windmill.
