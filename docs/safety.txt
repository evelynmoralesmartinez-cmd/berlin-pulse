# Safety tips for visitors in Berlin. One topic per paragraph. DRAFT by Claude – to be verified by Eve.
# Consejos de seguridad en Berlín. Un tema por párrafo. BORRADOR de Claude – por verificar.

General safety in Berlin: Berlin is generally a safe city for tourists. As in any big city, visitors should take normal precautions, especially in crowded places and at night.

Pickpockets in Berlin: Pickpockets are active in crowded places such as Alexanderplatz, the Brandenburg Gate, Christmas markets and the U-Bahn and S-Bahn. Keep your bag closed and in front of you, and do not keep your phone or wallet in your back pocket.

Tourist scams in Berlin: Near the main sights, especially the Brandenburg Gate, be careful with people asking you to sign a petition or playing the shell game (hütchenspiel). These are common tricks to get money from tourists.

Areas to be careful in Berlin at night: Some areas, such as Görlitzer Park, Kottbusser Tor and parts of Alexanderplatz, are known for petty crime and drug dealing, especially at night. It is better to avoid walking alone there late at night.

Bike lanes in Berlin: Berlin has many bike lanes, often on the pavement and marked in red. Pedestrians should not walk on them, because cyclists ride fast.

Public transport tickets in Berlin: There are no barriers in Berlin's public transport, but inspectors in plain clothes check tickets. Paper tickets must be validated in the machines on the platform before you ride. The fine for travelling without a valid ticket is €60.

Emergency numbers in Berlin: Emergency, ambulance and fire brigade: 112. Police: 110. Medical on-call service for non-emergencies: 116 117.

Health and pharmacies in Berlin: Medicines are sold in pharmacies (Apotheke). At night and on Sundays, an emergency pharmacy (Notdienst-Apotheke) is always open; every pharmacy shows on its door which one is open nearby.

Sundays in Berlin: Most shops and supermarkets in Berlin are closed on Sundays. Shops in main train stations, such as Hauptbahnhof, and small kiosks (Spätis) are usually open.
