# Neighbourhoods to stay in Berlin. Areas, not hotels. Verified by Eve.

Where to stay in Berlin – Mitte: Mitte is the historic centre of Berlin, close to most main sights such as the Brandenburg Gate and Museum Island. It is very well connected, but usually more expensive. Good for first-time visitors.

Where to stay in Berlin – Prenzlauer Berg: Prenzlauer Berg, in the Pankow district, is a calm and green neighbourhood with old buildings, cafés and many playgrounds. Good for families and couples.

Where to stay in Berlin – Kreuzberg: Kreuzberg is a diverse and creative neighbourhood, known for its street art, international food and nightlife along the canal. Good for young travellers and food lovers.

Where to stay in Berlin – Friedrichshain: Friedrichshain, in former East Berlin, is known for its clubs, bars and the East Side Gallery. It is usually cheaper than Mitte. Good for young travellers and nightlife.

Where to stay in Berlin – Charlottenburg: Charlottenburg, in former West Berlin, is an elegant and quieter neighbourhood near Kurfürstendamm, with classic shops, restaurants and Charlottenburg Palace. Good for travellers who want a calm and classic atmosphere.

Tips for booking accommodation in Berlin: Choose a place close to an U-Bahn or S-Bahn station. Book early during big events, such as the Berlin Marathon in September or large trade fairs. Berlin charges a city tax (Übernachtungsteuer) on the price of the room.
