# Basic facts about Berlin. One fact per paragraph. Verified by Eve.

Berlin is the capital of Germany and also one of its 16 federal states.

Population of Berlin: On 30 June 2026, Berlin had a total population of 3,917,196 inhabitants. Source: Amt für Statistik Berlin-Brandenburg (statistik-berlin-brandenburg.de).

Currency in Berlin: Germany uses the Euro (€). Card payment is accepted in many places in Berlin, but cash is still essential, because many shops, cafés and restaurants only accept cash.

Language in Berlin: German is the official language. English is the most widely used language in Berlin after German.

Public transport in Berlin – overview: Berlin has the U-Bahn (underground), the S-Bahn (city train), trams and buses. The city is divided into three ticket zones: A, B and C. The city centre is in zones A and B.

Public transport in Berlin – where to buy tickets: You can buy tickets at the ticket machines in U-Bahn and S-Bahn stations and at the airport, or in the BVG app.

Public transport in Berlin – single ticket: A single ticket for the city centre (zones AB) costs €4.00 and is valid for two hours.

Public transport in Berlin – airport ticket: A ticket from the airport to the city centre of Berlin costs €5.00.

Public transport in Berlin – 24-hour ticket: The 24-hour ticket for the city centre (zones AB) costs €11.20.

Public transport in Berlin – 72-hour ticket: If you stay in Berlin for three days, the best option is the Berlin WelcomeCard 72h. It costs €39.50 for zones AB (city centre) and €45.50 for zones ABC (including the airport). It includes unlimited public transport and discounts of 25% to 50% at more than 180 attractions.

Main sights in Berlin: The most important sights in Berlin include the Brandenburg Gate, the Reichstag, the Memorial to the Murdered Jews of Europe, Museum Island, Berlin Cathedral, the Humboldt Forum, the TV Tower, Alexanderplatz, the Nikolai Quarter, Gendarmenmarkt, Bebelplatz, the Neue Wache, Unter den Linden, Checkpoint Charlie, the Topography of Terror, Potsdamer Platz, Tiergarten, the Victory Column, the East Side Gallery, the Berlin Wall Memorial, the Oberbaum Bridge, Hackescher Markt, Charlottenburg Palace, the Kaiser Wilhelm Memorial Church, Kurfürstendamm, KaDeWe, Tempelhofer Feld, the Jewish Museum, the DDR Museum, the Soviet War Memorial in Treptower Park, Mauerpark, the Berlin Zoo and Teufelsberg. A popular day trip is Sanssouci Palace in Potsdam.

Best time to visit Berlin: The best times to visit Berlin are from May to July and from September to October.

Time zone of Berlin: Berlin uses Central European Time (CET, UTC+1) in winter and Central European Summer Time (CEST, UTC+2) in summer. Clocks move forward one hour on the last Sunday of March and back one hour on the last Sunday of October.

Tipping in Berlin: In Berlin, and in the rest of Germany, tipping is not mandatory. However, it is usual and polite to add between 5% and 10% of the total bill in restaurants and cafés.
