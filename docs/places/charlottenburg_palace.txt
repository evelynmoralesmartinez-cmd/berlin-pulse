# Detailed information about Charlottenburg Palace – New Wing. Source: spsg.de (original text in German)
# Información detallada sobre el Palacio de Charlottenburg – Ala Nueva. Fuente: spsg.de (texto original en alemán)

Charlottenburg Palace – New Wing – history: After coming to power in 1740, Frederick the Great had Sanssouci Palace built in Potsdam, and also the New Wing of Charlottenburg Palace as an extension of the Old Palace (Altes Schloss). The New Wing was almost completely destroyed during the Second World War and was largely rebuilt.

Charlottenburg Palace – New Wing – state rooms: The highlights of the New Wing are its impressive state rooms. The White Hall and the magnificent Golden Gallery are among the finest Rococo creations in Europe.

Charlottenburg Palace – New Wing – Frederick the Great's apartments: Besides the state rooms, two separate apartments were built for Frederick the Great, a king who loved music and the arts. Today these rooms house one of the largest collections of 18th-century French painting outside France, including masterpieces by Antoine Watteau.

Charlottenburg Palace – New Wing – Frederick William II: Frederick William II, the nephew and successor of Frederick the Great, had a summer apartment in the New Wing, decorated in the Chinoiserie and Etruscan styles, and winter rooms in the early Neoclassical style. Between 1788 and 1791, he completed the palace ensemble at the Great Orangery with a separate theatre building designed by Karl Gotthard Langhans.

Charlottenburg Palace – New Wing – Queen Luise: King Frederick William III and his famous wife, Queen Luise, also used the New Wing as their favourite summer residence. The queen lived in the former winter rooms of her father-in-law, on the upper floor of the west side. Her elegant bedroom, a fine example of Prussian furniture design, was designed by Karl Friedrich Schinkel in 1810. The former apartment of Frederick William III on the ground floor now shows an important collection of royal art from the early 19th century, including major works of French painting.

Charlottenburg Palace – New Wing – sculpture: In the entrance hall of the New Wing, a collection of Classical-Romantic sculptures shows the golden age of Berlin sculpture.
